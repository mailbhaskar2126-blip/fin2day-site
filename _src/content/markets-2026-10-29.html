Two days of losses ended in one strong session on Thursday. A day after the US Federal Reserve left interest rates unchanged, Indian shares climbed through the afternoon and closed near the day's high. The Sensex added more than 900 points.

  • Sensex74,151.04, +905.01 pts (+1.24%). Day's high 74,166.16.
  • Bank Nifty54,910, up 642 points, per Free Press Journal.

The Nifty opened slightly lower at 22,690.65 and dipped early before buyers took over. Analysts noted that the index climbed back above its 20-day moving average, a short-term trend line many traders track. New to the indices? See how the Sensex and Nifty work.

A broad rally, with autos left out

Sector and index gains, % change on 29 October 2026. Nifty Auto (−0.24%) was the only sector index to fall. Source: The Hans India, ChiniMandi, Business Standard.

Realty was the best-performing sector, with DLF up more than 4%. FMCG stocks rallied on what Geojit's Vinod Nair called "resilient consumer demand", and both public and private sector banks gained about 2%. Nifty Auto was the only sectoral index to close lower, as Maruti Suzuki, Bajaj Auto and Hero MotoCorp slipped.

Why the market rose

  • The Fed's pauseThe US Federal Reserve kept its policy rate at 3.75% to 4% on Wednesday. Our explainer on the Fed's hold covers what it means for India.
  • Softer oilBrent crude futures fell 1.03% to $114.13 a barrel, per PTI, on hopes for US-Iran diplomatic talks. Oil is still high: see why costly oil hurts India.
  • ResultsAdani Group shares rose 3% to 6% after strong September-quarter results from Adani Enterprises and Adani Ports.
  • Institutional buyingBoth foreign and domestic institutions were net buyers in the previous session.

Adani Ports led the Nifty with a 6.29% gain, followed by Adani Enterprises (+5.17%) and Kotak Mahindra Bank (+4.32%). Trent, Maruti Suzuki and Bajaj Auto fell the most.

Outside the Nifty, results drove some sharp moves. Birlasoft jumped about 12% and V-Guard Industries about 8% after their results. Hexaware Technologies fell about 6% after cutting its margin guidance, and RR Kabel fell about 4% after trimming its volume outlook. Equitas Small Finance Bank slumped after its MD and CEO, P. N. Vasudevan, resigned: Free Press Journal put the fall at 12%, and Business Standard reported a plunge of nearly 20% during the session. Jio Platforms, in its second day of trading, swung around but ended marginally higher.

Rupee and bonds

The rupee rose 3 paise to close at ₹95.25 to the dollar (provisional), per PTI. It had slid to 95.38 during the day, its weakest in about two weeks, as a firm dollar and demand from importers weighed. Softer crude and the rally in shares helped it recover. Dealers expect it to stay in a narrow range in the near term.

Geojit's Nair flagged the risks that remain: high input costs, crude prices, the weak rupee, rising domestic bond yields and geopolitical uncertainty.
