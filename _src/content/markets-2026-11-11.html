Indian shares picked up where they left off before Tuesday's Diwali-Balipratipada holiday. On Wednesday, 11 November, the Sensex rose 772.75 points, or 0.95%, to 81,879.40. The Nifty 50 gained 210.40 points, or 0.84%, to 25,255.05. DSIJ called it the indices' strongest showing since late February 2026, and HDFC Sky noted both are up nearly 10% in November so far.

The day had two halves. Cheaper oil and steady foreign buying sent stocks sharply higher at the open. Then a hot US inflation report pulled some of those gains back.

A big open, a softer close

Nifty 50 levels through the day, 11 November 2026. Times are IST; the first point is the 9 November close, the second the open. Source: DSIJ intraday updates, Angel One, HDFC Sky.

The Nifty opened at 25,337.80, almost 300 points above Monday's close. It touched a high of 25,424.35, up 1.52%, according to Angel One and Upstox. The Sensex reached 82,520.40 at its peak. By 2 pm the Nifty had slipped to 25,172.05 before recovering into the close. Its low for the day was 25,095.35. Breadth was firm: 37 of the 50 Nifty stocks rose.

Why stocks rose

  • OilBrent crude settled at $91.49 a barrel on Tuesday, its lowest settlement since August, after US President Donald Trump said the war with Iran could end soon. It briefly dipped below $90 that day for the first time since August. India imports most of its oil, so cheaper crude helps the rupee, inflation and company costs. See what $100 oil does to India.
  • Foreign moneyForeign investors bought a net ₹53,739 crore of Indian shares in the first nine days of November, per depositories data cited by PTI. That follows net selling of ₹34,843 crore in October and ₹35,861 crore in September. On Monday alone, FIIs bought ₹7,212.22 crore while domestic institutions sold ₹7,172.53 crore. Background: why FPIs and DIIs often pull in opposite directions.
  • Banks and autosState-run lenders and carmakers led the buying.

Banks and autos led, IT lagged

Daily % change of NSE sector and broader indices, 11 November 2026. Nifty 50 highlighted for reference. Source: DSIJ, Kotak Neo, Upstox.

The Nifty PSU Bank index rose 2.91%, the best sector of the day. The Bank Nifty added 1.76%, per Kotak Neo. Smaller companies did better than large ones: the Nifty Smallcap 100 rose 1.59% to 19,489.35, and the Midcap 100 rose 0.81% to 61,519.10. IT, pharma and realty slipped as investors moved money into domestic businesses.

Top five gainers and losers in the Nifty 50, 11 November 2026, % change. Source: HDFC Sky, Angel One.

Mahindra & Mahindra was the top Nifty stock, up 5.30% to ₹3,465, and added about 34 points to the index on its own, per DSIJ. IndiGo and Trent followed. ICICI Bank fell 1.10% and was the biggest drag, taking about 27 points off the Nifty. Adani Ports was the weakest Nifty stock, down 1.84%.

Results moved some mid-sized names. Kalyan Jewellers jumped 8.63% after its September-quarter profit rose 64% to ₹427 crore. Cochin Shipyard fell 6.82% after its quarterly profit halved to ₹54 crore. IIFL Finance rose 6% after its board approved a ₹6,414 crore preferential share issue, including ₹5,000 crore from Blackstone, per Upstox.

The brake: US inflation

US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October and core prices 0.3%, the US Bureau of Labor Statistics said on Tuesday. Both were above forecasts. US two-year Treasury yields jumped 17 basis points to 4.73%, per DSIJ, and futures markets now fully price a quarter-point Federal Reserve rate hike in December. Higher US rates tend to pull money back towards America, which is why the rally faded after midday. The Fed link is explained in how a Fed hike pulls money out of India.

Rupee and oil

The rupee rose 7 paise to a provisional 93.70 per dollar, PTI reported, helped by foreign inflows and cheaper oil. A stronger dollar, with the dollar index up 0.27% at 102.50, capped its gains. Brent crude bounced back on Wednesday and was at $92.41 a barrel in futures trade, up 1.01%. Attacks on shipping near the Strait of Hormuz keep supply risks alive.

What to watch

  • India CPI, 12 NovemberOctober retail inflation is due on Thursday. An Informist poll of economists expects about 6%, up from 5.54% in September, per Business Today. That would be the highest since October 2024. Background: September's inflation story.
  • The FedUS producer prices are next. The November US CPI is due on 10 December, just ahead of the Fed's December decision.
  • Oil and IranAny sign the US-Iran conflict is ending, or new attacks on ships, will move crude quickly.
  • ResultsThe September-quarter earnings season continues, with Tata Power and Garden Reach Shipbuilders among those reporting on Wednesday.
