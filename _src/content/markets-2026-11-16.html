Asian markets had a rough Monday. India did not. On 16 November, the Nifty 50 rose 146.80 points, or 0.58%, to 25,291.10. The Sensex gained 512.34 points, or 0.63%, to 82,104.79. The Nifty moved between 25,172.10 and 25,337.60 during the day, according to Upstox.

That recovered almost all of Friday's 155-point fall (see our 13 November update). But the gain sat almost entirely in big, defensive names. Smaller stocks had one of their weaker days this month.

Back near the top of the range

Nifty 50 closing level, last six trading sessions (markets were shut on 10 November). Source: NSE, via FIN2DAY daily updates, Upstox.

The Nifty Bank index rose 541.80 points, or 0.89%, to 61,575.90, according to Liquide. The broader market went the other way. The Nifty Midcap 100 fell 0.51% to 61,576.40 and the Nifty Smallcap 100 dropped 1.10% to 19,441.10.

Breadth was poor. On the NSE, 1,175 stocks rose and 2,515 fell, Upstox data show. Inside the Nifty 50, though, 31 stocks rose and 19 fell. India VIX, the market's fear gauge, edged up 1.33% to 15.7. New to the indices? Read how the Sensex and Nifty work.

Why India rose while Asia fell

Elsewhere in Asia, shares fell on doubts about the AI trade. Upstox listed Taiwan down 6.4%, Korea's Kospi down 3.73% and Japan's Nikkei down 2.47%. HDFC Sky linked the sell-off to the shock exit of Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang and caution before Nvidia's results and an expected Anthropic IPO this week.

India has little exposure to those crowded AI trades, and that is now working in its favour. Foreign investors bought a net ₹5,736.55 crore of Indian shares on Friday, exchange data show, and have put in about $8.9 billion so far in November, according to HDFC Sky. Domestic institutions sold ₹2,253.58 crore on Friday. For why this tug-of-war matters, see how foreign and domestic flows move the market.

Oil helped too. Brent crude futures fell 1.03% to $94.85 a barrel, PTI reported, as shipments through the Strait of Hormuz recover. Cheaper oil eases India's import bill. The rupee rose 27 paise to close at 93.92 to the dollar (provisional), from 94.19 on Friday.

Consumer staples led, metals and pharma lagged

Nifty sectoral indices, percentage change on the day. Source: Upstox, Kotak Neo, Liquide market wraps.

Investors chose safe, steady businesses. The Nifty FMCG index jumped 1.87%, its best sector, with ITC up 3.54% and Tata Consumer up 3.57%. Private banks gained 0.88%. Pharma (−1.53%), metals (−1.50%), realty (−1.34%) and autos (−1.29%) all fell. Geojit's Vinod Nair cited weak global cues, high bond yields and profit-taking in mid- and small-caps, with money moving into banks and defensive sectors.

A results shock for Tata Motors PV

Biggest Nifty 50 gainers and losers, percentage change on the day. Source: Upstox, Kotak Neo market wraps.

Hospital stocks were strong: Max Healthcare rose 6.63% and Apollo Hospitals 2.87%. At the other end, Tata Motors PV fell 8.70% to ₹262.40 after its September-quarter results. HDFC Sky said Jaguar Land Rover cut its FY27 EBIT margin guidance to 2–3% from 4%. Tata Steel, Maruti Suzuki and Hindalco each lost more than 2%.

Also today: wholesale inflation at 12.29%

Wholesale (WPI) inflation rose to 12.29% in October from 11.39% in September, government data showed on Monday. Fuel and power prices were up 32.84% on a year earlier. Retail inflation was 6.09% in October. See what drove September's inflation.
