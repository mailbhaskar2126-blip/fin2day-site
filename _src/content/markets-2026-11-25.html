After Tuesday's market holiday, Dalal Street came back with a bounce. The Nifty 50 closed 469.60 points, or 1.85%, higher at 25,819.25. The Sensex rose 1,502.29 points, or 1.82%, to 83,938.28.

That recovered almost all of Monday's 450.95-point Nifty fall, covered in our 23 November update. It happened on a day when most of Asia was deep in the red.

A gap-up, then a steady day

Nifty 50 open, day high and close, in points above Monday's close of 25,349.65. FIN2DAY calculation. Source: HDFC Sky, Angel One.

The Nifty opened 308.70 points higher at 25,658.35 and touched 25,951.65, about 48 points short of 26,000. It gave back some of that but held most of the gain into the close. The Sensex hit 84,498.21 at its best.

India VIX, the market's fear gauge, eased 0.29% to 17.65.

Who led: consumer staples and banks

Nifty sector and broader index moves, % change on the day. Source: HDFC Sky, Kotak Neo, Marketcalls, Liquide, Upstox.

The FMCG index jumped 3.79%, the best sector on the day. Pharma rose 2.38% and private banks 2.21%. Bank Nifty gained 1.79%, or 1,117.90 points, to 63,703.25. IT added 1.12%.

Metals went the other way. The Metal index fell 2.04% and Media 4.44%. Tata Steel, which slid 8.43% on Monday, lost another 2.27%.

Top five Nifty 50 gainers and losers, % change on the day. Source: Upstox, HDFC Sky.

Hindustan Unilever was the star, up 6.08% to ₹2,141.80. Tata Consumer Products gained 4.66% and Nestle India 4.03%. HDFC Life rose 4.58% after the insurance regulator IRDAI set up an expert committee on insurance distribution reforms, Upstox reported.

A narrow rally

The gains were concentrated in large stocks. Of the 50 Nifty stocks, 42 rose and 8 fell, according to Angel One. But the Nifty Midcap 100 rose only 0.30% to 61,584.75, and the Nifty Smallcap 100 fell 0.44% to 19,229.35. HDFC Sky described overall market breadth as negative.

In other words, the indices rose mostly because big, heavily weighted stocks such as HUL and the banks rose. To see why a handful of stocks can move the whole index, read how the Sensex and Nifty work.

Why markets rose

  • Foreign buyingForeign institutional investors bought a net ₹5,672.99 crore of shares on Monday, per exchange data, while domestic institutions sold ₹2,760.12 crore, Upstox reported. See why these flows matter.
  • Cheaper oilBrent crude was at $90.30 a barrel, down 1.20%, according to HDFC Sky. India imports most of its oil, so lower prices help inflation and the rupee.
  • Weak Asia, ignoredKorea's Kospi fell 6.16%, Japan's Nikkei 2.78% and Shanghai 2.03%, per Marketcalls, as an AI-led sell-off in US tech stocks continued. US stock futures were also lower.

Rupee at an eight-month high

The rupee rose 69 paise to settle at 92.16 (provisional) against the US dollar, from 92.85 on Monday, PTI reported. It opened at 92.65. PTI said the rupee has now recouped all its losses of the current financial year, helped by foreign inflows and softer crude.

What to watch

  • US inflationThe US PCE inflation reading for October was due at 7 pm IST on Wednesday. Markets are watching it for clues on whether the Fed raises rates in December.
  • US holidayUS markets are shut on Thursday, 26 November, for Thanksgiving. NSE and BSE trade as usual.
  • US tariffsHDFC Sky reported that Washington is preparing higher tariffs on China, the EU and 14 other economies, with India and Vietnam possibly facing higher duties.
  • RBI policyThe RBI's next monetary policy decision is on 4 December.
  • LevelsOptions data cited by Marketcalls show the biggest call open interest at 26,000 and put bases at 25,500 and 25,000 for the 1 December weekly expiry.
