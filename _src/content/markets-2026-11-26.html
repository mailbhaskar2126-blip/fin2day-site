Indian shares ended a choppy Thursday a little higher, carried almost entirely by IT stocks. The Sensex rose 390.31 points, or 0.46%, to 84,328.59. The Nifty 50 gained 65.25 points, or 0.25%, to 25,884.50.

That came on top of Wednesday's 1,502-point Sensex jump, covered in our 25 November update. But today's gain was narrow: 22 Nifty stocks rose and 28 fell, according to Angel One.

Close to 26,000, then a slip

Nifty 50 open, day high, day low and close, in points versus Wednesday's close of 25,819.25. FIN2DAY calculation. Source: Angel One, HDFC Sky.

The Nifty opened at 25,873.20 and climbed to 25,974.95, about 25 points short of 26,000. In the afternoon it fell to 25,733.50, below Wednesday's close, before recovering into the bell. The Sensex touched 84,551.74 at its best, Upstox reported. Marketcalls attributed the fade from the highs to profit booking.

IT and realty up, consumer stocks down

Nifty sector and broader market indices, % change on the day. Source: HDFC Sky, Liquide, Marketcalls, Upstox.

The Nifty IT index jumped 3.66% and Nifty Realty 3.27%. Metals added 1.17%. On the other side, consumer durables fell 1.33%, FMCG 0.94% and autos 0.69%. Bank Nifty slipped 0.26% to 63,535.30, after touching 64,104 in the morning.

Smaller companies did better than on Wednesday. The Nifty Midcap 100 rose 0.32% to 61,781.85 and the Nifty Smallcap 100 0.74% to 19,372.15, per Upstox. HDFC Sky counted 2,140 advancing shares against 1,903 declining.

Who moved the index

Top five Nifty 50 gainers and losers, % change on the day. Source: Angel One, HDFC Sky, Upstox.

Four of the five biggest Nifty gainers were IT companies. HCL Technologies rose 6.42% to ₹1,468.60, TCS 4.89% and Infosys 4.69%. Stock exchange operator BSE gained 5.70%. The losers were mostly consumer and finance names: Asian Paints fell 2.98%, Bajaj Finance 2.96% and Shriram Finance 2.84%.

Because IT companies carry a large weight in the Nifty, a sharp move in a few of them can lift the whole index even when more stocks fall than rise. Read how the Sensex and Nifty work.

Why IT rallied

  • US growthUS third-quarter GDP growth was revised up to 4.3% annualised from 3.4%, HDFC Sky reported. Marketcalls said strong US data lifted hopes of higher tech spending. Indian IT firms earn much of their revenue in the US.
  • AI worriesThe Week reported that investors turned to traditional Indian IT companies amid concerns about heavy AI spending elsewhere.
  • Foreign buyingForeign institutional investors bought a net ₹4,479.08 crore of shares on Wednesday and domestic institutions ₹4,018.72 crore, per exchange data cited by Upstox. See why these flows matter.

What held markets back

  • US inflation and the FedUS PCE inflation was 3.8% in October, with core at 3.3%, and markets are pricing a quarter-point Fed rate hike at its 8–9 December meeting, HDFC Sky said. Read what a global rate wave means for India.
  • TariffsProposed new US tariffs on several trading partners, including India, kept investors cautious. HDFC Sky and Marketcalls said hearings are set for 25 January.
  • OilBrent crude was trading at $93.80 a barrel, down 0.86%, in futures trade, PTI reported, but West Asia tensions after Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia kept supply worries alive.

Rupee gives back some gains

The rupee fell 24 paise to settle at 92.44 (provisional) against the US dollar, from 92.20 on Wednesday, PTI reported. It moved between 92.31 and 92.66 during the day. Dealers cited higher crude, tariff fears and inflation concerns, while foreign inflows offered support. Wednesday's close was the rupee's strongest this financial year.
