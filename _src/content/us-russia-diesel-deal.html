For a year, Washington told New Delhi one thing: stop buying Russian oil. On 9 October 2026, President Trump announced the US would buy Russian diesel instead.

How India got squeezed

In August 2025 the US put a 25% reciprocal tariff on Indian goods, then added another 25% as a penalty for buying Russian crude. That took the total to 50%. In February 2026, India agreed to stop buying Russian crude, and the US said it would cut the tariff to 18%.

US tariffs on Indian goods. The Sep 2026 law allows tariffs of up to 100% on the five biggest buyers of Russian oil and gas; final rates are still to be set.

Then on 18 September 2026, Trump signed a new sanctions law. It directs tariffs of up to 100% on the top five importers of Russian oil or gas, which puts India back in the line of fire.

Why Trump did it

  • $6.52Record US national average for diesel per AAA, on 22 September 2026. It was $6.28 on 9 October.
  • MidtermsTrump said lower fuel prices for truckers, farmers and ranchers were his top priority, with midterm elections weeks away.
  • RussiaMoscow had restricted diesel exports in July after Ukrainian attacks on its refineries. It said it would lift those limits early.

The reaction

Ukraine's President Zelenskyy called it "a weak decision by strong partners." US Democrats including Chuck Schumer and Elizabeth Warren called it a betrayal of Ukraine. Analysts disagreed on whether it would bring prices down much at all.

What it means for India

The same law that threatens India with up to 100% tariffs is still on the books. Watch for which five countries the US Trade Representative names, and whether India's February deal protects it.
