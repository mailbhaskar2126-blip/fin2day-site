Wall Street got back much of what it lost. In a holiday-shortened week to Friday 27 November, the S&P 500 rose 5.6%, its biggest weekly gain since April 2025. The Nasdaq Composite gained 6.7% and the Dow Jones 5.5%, its best week in four years, Investopedia reported. A week earlier, all three had suffered their worst week in more than 18 months.

Here is what changed, and what it could mean for Indian markets on Monday.

The week in US stocks

  • S&P 5007,297.49 on Friday, up 1.6% on the day and 5.6% for the week.
  • Nasdaq Composite24,129.50, up 2.3% on Friday and 6.7% for the week, its best week since May 2025.
  • Dow Jones50,774.94, up 591.99 points on Friday and 5.5% for the week.
Weekly change in the main US indexes, week to 27 November 2026. Source: Investopedia.

It was not a smooth ride. Stocks rallied on Monday, swung sharply on Tuesday and Wednesday, and the market was shut on Thursday for Thanksgiving, AP reported. Friday was a half-day session that ended at 1 pm New York time. Every one of the S&P 500's 11 sectors rose on Friday, and the VIX "fear gauge" fell 11% to 23.81.

Last week's fall and this week's rebound in the S&P 500 and Nasdaq. Source: AP (week to 20 Nov), Investopedia (week to 27 Nov).

The rebound did not fully undo the damage. The S&P 500 and Dow had each fallen about 8% the week before, and the Nasdaq 10%. A 10% fall needs a gain of about 11% to recover, so the indexes are still below where they started the previous week (FIN2DAY calculation).

What drove the bounce

The biggest story was Nvidia. Its shares jumped 31.48% on Monday after Jensen Huang returned as chief executive, StockTitan reported, though they gave back part of that gain later in the week. Nvidia closed Friday up 0.8% at $150.49, still 37% below its October record high, according to Investopedia.

Other chip stocks led Friday's rally. Micron and Marvell rose more than 7%, Intel and AMD about 5%, and Broadcom 4%. Memory-chip maker SanDisk soared 16%, the S&P 500's top gainer. Anthropic, which listed last week in the largest IPO ever, rose 12% to just under $92, back above its $83 IPO price after two sessions below it.

Bonds, the dollar and the Fed

Traders now see a December rate hike by the Federal Reserve as all but certain. CME FedWatch showed a 99.2% chance of a quarter-point hike on 9 December, Investing.com reported. That would lift the Fed's target range from 3.75%–4% to 4%–4.25%. A key reason: core PCE inflation, the Fed's preferred gauge, rose 3.3% in the year to October, its fastest pace since November 2023.

The 10-year US Treasury yield rose to 4.94% on Friday from 4.86%, though it is still below the 5.15% it reached on 13 November. The dollar index rose 0.25% to 104.04 on Friday and was set for a small weekly gain. It touched 104.53 on Monday, its highest level since March 2025. Gold futures rose 2.8% to about $3,635 an ounce on Friday, bouncing off a 14-month low.

Why it matters for India

Indian shares had a strong week until Friday, then fell as Asia rallied. On Friday the Sensex lost 989.33 points to 83,339.26 and the Nifty 50 fell 1.07% to 25,607.20, while Korea, China and Japan all rose more than 2%. Our 27 November markets update has the details.

  • Foreign moneyForeign investors have been buying Indian shares: a net ₹4,479.08 crore on Wednesday and ₹5,843.33 crore on Thursday. A calmer Wall Street usually supports those flows.

What to watch next week

The Fed's quiet period before its 9 December decision begins on Saturday, so officials will stop commenting on policy. In the US, job openings data comes on Tuesday, 1 December, and the November jobs report on Friday, 4 December. In India, the RBI's December policy meeting is close too, after its recent efforts to defend the rupee.
