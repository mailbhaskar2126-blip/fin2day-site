About

Finance news is written for people who already understand it.

FIN2DAY is for everyone else.

Every day there's a headline about the repo rate, an IPO, a tariff or an oil shock. Most coverage assumes you already know what those words mean and why they matter. FIN2DAY starts from the other end: what happened, why, and what it means for your EMI, your savings and your job.

What we promise

  • One storyThe one that matters most each day, not a feed of twenty.
  • Real numbersEvery figure comes from a primary source or an established news agency, and we list them.
  • No tipsWe explain. We never tell you what to buy or sell.
  • Both sidesOn contested topics, we lay out the facts and the main arguments, and let you decide.

Where to find us

Daily carousels and reels on Instagram @fin2day, full explainers here, and a weekday-morning newsletter.
