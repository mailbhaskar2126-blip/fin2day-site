Nifty reclaims 23,000 on an IT rally, but two of every three stocks fell

Infosys, Trent and IndiGo lifted the benchmarks for a second straight day. Underneath, midcaps and smallcaps fell and decliners outnumbered gainers more than two to one.

The headline numbers said "up" for a second day running. The Nifty closed back above 23,000 and the Sensex added 308 points. But the gains came from a handful of large companies, led by IT stocks. Across the wider market, far more shares fell than rose.

  • Nifty 5023,053.10, +116.35 pts (+0.51%). Opened at 22,964 and touched 23,118 at the high.
  • Sensex74,459.53, +308.49 pts (+0.42%).
  • Bank Nifty54,686.95, −222.70 pts (−0.41%).
  • Midcap / SmallcapNifty Midcap 100 −0.93%; Nifty Smallcap 100 −1.85%.

According to 5paisa, the Nifty ended the week about 2% higher, making up the previous week's losses. Thursday's jump after the US Fed's pause is covered in yesterday's update.

A narrow rally

Rising vs falling stocks on 30 October 2026. Source: HDFC Sky.

The Nifty Midcap 100 fell 0.93% and the Smallcap 100 1.85%, while Bank Nifty lost 0.41%. Market breadth, the count of rising versus falling shares, was weak. HDFC Sky reported 1,311 stocks advancing, 2,862 declining and 170 unchanged. That is more than two decliners for every gainer. The Nifty and Sensex can still rise on such a day because they are weighted by company size: a big move in a few heavyweights like Infosys can outweigh losses in hundreds of smaller stocks. Our explainer on how the Sensex and Nifty work shows why.

The India VIX, a measure of expected volatility, rose about 2%, per 5paisa.

IT and pharma carried the day

Sector index moves, % change on 30 October 2026. Source: Liquide; Nifty Media from 5paisa.

Nifty IT gained 2.32%, its best showing among sectors. 5paisa linked the buying to positive sentiment after US peer Cognizant's quarterly earnings. Wipro and Coforge rose more than 4%, Infosys 3.36% and HCL Technologies 1.72%. Pharma added 1.55%.

Most other sectors slipped. Consumer durables fell 1.78% and media 1.46%. Energy, auto, realty, FMCG and metal stocks were all lower, and banks gave back part of Thursday's rally.

Results moved the biggest stocks

Biggest Nifty 50 gainers and losers, % change on 30 October 2026. Source: HDFC Sky, Liquide.
  • TrentUp 5.44% to ₹3,017.30. Its September-quarter revenue rose 22.5% to ₹5,788 crore and net profit 8.6% to ₹490 crore, per 5paisa.
  • IndiGoUp 4.33% to ₹4,877.60 after its quarterly loss narrowed to ₹1,879 crore from ₹2,581.7 crore a year earlier.
  • Coal IndiaDown 1.04% after its September-quarter profit missed expectations, per HDFC Sky.
  • Outside the NiftySuzlon Energy fell about 7.9% on weak operating margins and lower brokerage targets.

Shriram Finance (−2.13%), Nestlé India (−1.82%) and Mahindra & Mahindra (−1.72%) were the biggest Nifty decliners.

Global cues, oil and the rupee

The backdrop was shaky. Global technology shares fell on worries about returns from AI spending, and Asian markets including Japan and South Korea weakened, HDFC Sky reported. Nasdaq 100 futures were down 2.39% during the Indian afternoon, per 5paisa.

Oil stayed expensive. Brent crude's January contract was at $113.09 a barrel around 3:35 pm IST, Kotak Neo reported, as markets watched US-Iran tensions. High oil prices raise India's import bill, which is why they matter for the rupee and inflation.

The rupee strengthened. It rose 33 paise to close at a provisional ₹94.93 to the dollar, from ₹95.26, PTI reported. Traders credited a softer dollar and RBI intervention.

Who was buying

Foreign investors turned sellers again on Thursday, 29 October, with net sales of ₹943.60 crore in the cash market. Domestic institutions bought ₹1,924.41 crore, according to Upstox data. Friday's figures were not yet available at the time of writing.

What to watch

Reliance Industries, the Nifty's heaviest stock, was due to report September-quarter results after market hours on Friday; its shares closed 0.21% lower at ₹1,164.90 ahead of the numbers. The Bombay Coated and Special Steels IPO (₹78–82 a share) closes on 2 November. Beyond that, traders are tracking Brent above $110, whether foreign selling resumes, US tech earnings and the US midterm elections. Liquide flagged 23,000 as near-term support for the Nifty.
