Reliance jumps 11.6% on record profit, lifting Nifty 1.68% to 23,440 as oil cools

One stock did about half the work. Reliance's best day since April 2020, a 3% drop in Brent and foreign buying pushed the Sensex up 1,233 points to start Diwali week.

Diwali week opened with a bang on Dalal Street. The Nifty 50 rose 387.70 points, or 1.68%, to 23,440.80 on Monday, 2 November. The Sensex gained 1,233.25 points, or 1.66%, to 75,692.78, close to a two-month high, according to HDFC Sky. It was the third straight day of gains for both benchmarks.

Unlike Friday, when the Nifty rose but most stocks fell, this rally was broad. On the NSE, 2,668 stocks advanced and 1,469 declined. Bank Nifty added 315.25 points, or 0.58%, to 55,002.20. The Nifty Midcap 100 rose 1.19% and the Smallcap 100 0.71%.

One stock, half the move

Reliance Industries jumped 11.6% to ₹1,300, its biggest one-day gain since April 2020, after reporting its highest-ever quarterly profit, ahead of analyst estimates. Refining and petrochemical margins did the heavy lifting: IIFL reports that earnings from the oil-to-chemicals business rose 67.9% from a year earlier to ₹25,203 crore. Jio Platforms' revenue grew 11.3%. Brokerages flagged weaker performance in the retail business.

Reliance is the largest stock in the Nifty, with a weight of about 7.3%, per IIFL. A rough FIN2DAY calculation (7.3% weight × 11.6% gain) suggests Reliance alone added about 0.85 percentage points, roughly half of the Nifty's 1.68% rise. To see why one heavyweight can move an index this much, read how the Sensex and Nifty work.

Metal stocks followed. Tata Steel rose 5.83% and JSW Steel 5.13%. Coal India and Shriram Finance each added 4.88%. The losers' list was mild: Infosys fell 2.03%, and three private banks and Dr Reddy's slipped less than 1%.

The five biggest Nifty 50 losers on 2 November. Source: IIFL.

Energy and metals led, IT lagged

Sector and broader indices that rose on 2 November. Source: IIFL, Kotak Neo (Oil & Gas, Midcap 100, Smallcap 100).

Nine of 13 major sector indices closed higher. The Nifty Oil & Gas index rose 5.95%, with Reliance, ONGC (+4.19%) and Indian Oil (+3.4%) all higher. IT, media and pharma were the only notable decliners, each down less than 1%.

The sector indices that fell on 2 November. Source: IIFL.

Oil, the rupee and global cues

Crude gave markets a second push. Brent fell 3.48% to $109.95 a barrel in afternoon trade, per PTI, down from a six-month high of $118 last week. Iranian and Qatari officials held a third day of talks aimed at ending the US-Israeli war on Iran. Cheaper oil helps India, which imports most of its crude; see what $100 oil does to India.

The rupee did not join the rally. It closed at a provisional ₹94.86 to the dollar, down 10 paise, as Asian currencies weakened. Overseas, the Nasdaq fell nearly 2.9% on Friday on worries about AI spending, and IIFL notes some analysts see India gaining from money rotating out of US tech. The US 10-year Treasury yield slipped to 4.927%.

Who was buying

Foreign institutional investors were net buyers of ₹3,598.54 crore on Friday, while domestic institutions sold ₹817.51 crore. That is a reversal of the usual pattern this year. For all of October, FIIs sold a net ₹45,161.71 crore and DIIs bought ₹62,220.11 crore. Read why foreigners sell while Indian funds buy.

  • India dataFinal services PMI on 4 November and October auto sales during the week.
  • USMidterm elections on 3 November and the US jobs report on 6 November.
