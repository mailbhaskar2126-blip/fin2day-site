Nestle, Tata Consumer lead an FMCG rush as Sensex adds 1,225 points; Nifty ends at 23,811

Consumer stocks took the baton from Reliance. The Nifty rose 1.58% for a fourth straight gain, while SBI slipped despite a 14% jump in profit.

The rally kept going on Tuesday, 3 November, but with a different engine. The Nifty 50 rose 370.05 points, or 1.58%, to 23,810.85. The Sensex gained 1,225.07 points, or 1.62%, to 76,917.85. It was the fourth straight day of gains for both, and their highest levels in nearly two months, according to HDFC Sky.

On Monday, one stock, Reliance, did about half the Nifty's work (see yesterday's update). On Tuesday the gains were spread out: 39 of the 50 Nifty stocks closed higher, per Angel One. The Nifty Midcap 100 rose 1.06% and the Smallcap 100 0.31%.

Nifty 50 closing levels over the last six sessions. Source: NSE via FIN2DAY's daily updates, HDFC Sky.

Since its close of 22,698.85 on 28 October, the Nifty has gained 1,112 points, or about 4.9% in four sessions (FIN2DAY calculation).

Consumer stocks take the lead

The Nifty FMCG index was the day's best sector, up 3.65%, according to 5paisa and Kotak Neo. Nestle India rose 4.47% and Tata Consumer Products 4.29%. Outside the Nifty, Marico jumped 10% after its September-quarter results: Upstox reports that consolidated net profit rose 31% to ₹550 crore, revenue grew 13%, and the company raised its FY27 guidance. HDFC Sky added that lower copra (dried coconut) costs helped.

Realty, private banks and autos also gained. Capital-market stocks were in demand too: BSE Ltd was the Nifty's top gainer, up 5.03%, and MCX rose 5.78%.

How NSE sector indices moved on 3 November.

SBI slips despite higher profit

Not everything rose. State Bank of India fell 1.70% to ₹970.25, even though its standalone net profit for the September quarter rose 14.28% to ₹23,037.95 crore, per Upstox. The Nifty PSU Bank index fell 1.27%, the weakest sector. Power Grid was the biggest Nifty loser, down 3.04%, followed by SBI, ONGC and Infosys.

The five biggest Nifty 50 gainers and losers on 3 November.

Why the market rose

  • OilBrent crude fell about 7% on Monday on hopes the US-Israeli war with Iran could end. It firmed slightly on Tuesday, trading at $105.46 a barrel around 3:36 pm, per Kotak Neo, after reports of US strikes on Iran's Qeshm Island.
  • Foreign moneyForeign institutional investors bought a net ₹3,114.68 crore of shares on Monday, while domestic institutions sold ₹392.20 crore (provisional NSE data). For why these two often pull in opposite directions, see FPIs sell, DIIs buy.
  • Earnings"Resilient Q2 earnings further strengthened confidence in the domestic growth outlook," said Vinod Nair of Geojit Investments.

The rupee closed 16 paise stronger at 94.74 to the dollar, from 94.90, according to HDFC Sky. Gold and silver futures on MCX fell 1.28% and 3.64%.

A Nifty gain this broad shows how an index reflects its biggest constituents together. For the mechanics, read how the Sensex and Nifty work.

What to watch

  • US midterm electionsAnalysts cited the US vote as a source of event risk for global markets this week.
  • Oil and IranAny escalation in the Gulf could quickly reverse the oil relief that has helped Indian shares and the rupee.
  • Diwali calendarMuhurat trading runs on Sunday, 8 November, from 2:20 pm to 3:30 pm. Markets are shut on Tuesday, 10 November, for Diwali Balipratipada.
  • ResultsThe September-quarter earnings season continues. Some analysts see resistance for the Nifty around 23,800 to 23,900.
