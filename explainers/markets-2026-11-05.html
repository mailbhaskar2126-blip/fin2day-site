Sensex soars 2,009 points as oil slides; Nifty's sixth straight gain takes it to 24,537

The benchmarks had their biggest one-day rally in seven months as Brent crude tumbled and foreign investors kept buying. Banks, finance companies and carmakers led.

Indian shares had their best day in seven months on Thursday, 5 November. The Sensex jumped 2,008.61 points, or 2.60%, to 79,310.01. The Nifty 50 rose 576 points, or 2.40%, to 24,536.60. Upstox called it the biggest rally for the indices in seven months.

It was the sixth gain in a row for both. HDFC Sky said that is their longest winning streak in a year, with the indices up more than 8% over the six sessions. Even so, the Nifty is still down 6.2% for 2026 and the Sensex 6.9%, per HDFC Sky.

Nifty 50 closing levels, 28 October to 5 November 2026. Source: NSE via FIN2DAY's daily updates, Upstox.

From its 28 October close of 22,698.85, the Nifty has gained 1,837.75 points, or 8.1% (FIN2DAY calculation). Wednesday's gain was led by smaller stocks (see yesterday's update). Today the giants did the lifting.

Oil did most of the work

The single biggest reason was crude oil. Brent fell as much as 11% to a low of $93.50 a barrel on Thursday, from an intraday high of $104.96 on Wednesday, according to Upstox. Traders bet that Democratic control of both houses of the US Congress would push the US towards a diplomatic end to the conflict with Iran. By the afternoon, Brent futures had bounced 1.38% to $95.93, PTI reported.

India imports most of its oil, so cheaper crude eases pressure on inflation, the trade deficit and the rupee all at once. Upstox also pointed to softer bond yields and better sentiment around West Asia.

Foreign money kept coming

Foreign institutional investors bought a net ₹7,284.03 crore of Indian shares on Wednesday, their fourth straight day of buying, per provisional data cited by Upstox and DSIJ. HDFC Sky said it was their biggest single-day purchase since October 2025. Domestic institutions sold ₹3,712.23 crore. For why the two groups often pull in opposite directions, read our explainer.

That buying showed up in heavyweight banks. ICICI Bank added about 119 points to the Sensex and HDFC Bank about 78, per DSIJ. Because these stocks carry big weights, they move the index far more than smaller names. See how the Sensex and Nifty work.

Finance and autos led; media and energy lagged

Fourteen of 16 major sector indices rose, per HDFC Sky. Nifty Capital Market gained 4.24% and Auto 4.14%. Financial Services rose 3.99%, Realty 3.53% and Private Bank 3.44%, per Upstox and Kotak Neo. Media (−0.84%) and Energy (about −0.3%) were the only losers. Oil producers such as ONGC fell as crude prices dropped.

How NSE sector indices moved on 5 November. Energy rounded to one decimal by the source. Source: Upstox, HDFC Sky, Kotak Neo.

The broader market rose too, but less than the benchmarks this time. The Nifty Midcap 100 gained 1.87% to 60,620.90 and the Nifty Smallcap 100 rose 0.56% to 19,132.45, per DSIJ and Kotak Neo. On the NSE, 2,547 stocks advanced and 1,084 declined. Within the Nifty, 39 rose and 11 fell. NSE-listed companies added ₹7.85 lakh crore in market value, per Upstox.

Biggest gainers and losers in the Nifty 50 on 5 November. Source: Angel One, HDFC Sky, DSIJ.
  • Top gainersBajaj Finance +7.65% (₹1,070), Shriram Finance +6.08% (₹964), Mahindra & Mahindra +5.59% (₹3,127.50), Maruti Suzuki +5.48%, InterGlobe Aviation +5.43%.
  • Top losersApollo Hospitals −2.66% (₹8,371), ONGC −2.13% (₹230), NTPC −1.63% (₹316.70), Coal India −1.34%, Adani Ports −1.33%.
  • Outside the NiftyAshok Leyland rose 10.5% and UNO Minda 11.5%. PI Industries fell 19.07% to ₹1,770, per HDFC Sky.

Rupee flat, volatility up

The rupee ended unchanged at 94.46 to the dollar (provisional), PTI reported. It opened at 94.36 and slipped to 94.57 during the day, as cheaper oil was offset by a firm dollar overseas. PTI put Wednesday's final settlement at 94.46, stronger than the provisional 94.58 in our previous update. The India VIX volatility index rose 2.5% to 15.57, per Upstox.

What to watch

  • OilToday's rally rests heavily on crude. Hariselvan Radhakrishnan of HST Wealth told Upstox that a rebound in oil or a reversal in foreign flows would suggest the move was driven by "temporary geopolitical relief".
  • LevelsThe same analyst said sustained trade above 24,330, with banks still leading and crude stable, could carry the Nifty towards 24,775.
  • ResultsTitan was due to report September-quarter results on 5 November, per DSIJ. Lenskart rose 10.6% in early trade after its quarterly profit more than doubled, per Business Standard.
  • After a big dayKotak Neo said the indices could see "some consolidation" in the next session after such a sharp rally.
