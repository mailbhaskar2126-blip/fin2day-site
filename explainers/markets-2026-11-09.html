Banks rescue a red day: Nifty ends at 25,044.65, an eight-month closing high, as PSU banks jump 3.7% and IT slips

The Nifty and Sensex spent most of Monday in the red after the Diwali Muhurat session, then a late push by bank stocks carried both to their highest closes since 27 February. More shares fell than rose.

For most of Monday, 9 November, it looked as if the Diwali rally had run out of steam. Then the banks stepped in. The Sensex closed 107.25 points, or 0.13%, higher at 81,106.65. The Nifty 50 rose 58.25 points, or 0.23%, to 25,044.65.

The gains are small, but the levels are not. Both indices closed at their highest since 27 February 2026, per HDFC Sky. They had already climbed about 10.5% over seven straight sessions to Friday. On Sunday's one-hour Muhurat session, the Nifty slipped 43.95 points to 24,986.40 and the Sensex 28.62 points to 80,999.40, so Monday's moves are measured from those levels.

Red most of the day, green at the bell

Sensex levels through the day, 9 November 2026. Times are IST; Prev is the close of the 8 November Muhurat session. Source: DSIJ intraday updates, BSE.

The Sensex opened weaker and was down 179.75 points at 9:20 am. It was still in the red at noon and at 2 pm. Its range for the day ran from a low of 80,519.31 to a high of 81,339.41, per DSIJ. The Nifty swung between 24,838.75 and 25,084.05 before closing near the top of that range.

Banks did the lifting

Percentage change in Nifty sector indices and broader indices, 9 November 2026. Source: NSE via DSIJ, HDFC Sky, Kotak Neo.

State-run lenders led. The Nifty PSU Bank index jumped 3.73%, and Bank Nifty rose 1.51% to 59,741.25. SBI gained 2.56%, the best of the Nifty 50, followed by Trent (+2%) and NTPC (+1.9%), per HDFC Sky and DSIJ.

Pharma was the other bright spot, up 1.65%. Divi's Laboratories was among companies reporting September-quarter results, with profit up 63.6% to ₹1,127 crore, DSIJ reported.

Three bank stocks alone added more than 108 Nifty points, more than the index's whole gain for the day (FIN2DAY calculation).

Points each stock added to or took off the Nifty 50, 9 November 2026. Biggest three on each side. Source: DSIJ.

IT and metals held it back

Nifty IT was the worst sector, down 1.22%. HCL Technologies fell 2.4%, Infosys 1.5% and Tech Mahindra 1.4%, per HDFC Sky. Tata Steel was the biggest Nifty loser, down 2.8%, on a day when it reported a 16.2% rise in quarterly profit to ₹3,699.59 crore. UltraTech Cement fell 1.8%, ITC 1.64% and Bajaj Finance 1.56%.

Underneath the headline gain, more stocks fell than rose. On the NSE, 2,316 shares declined and 1,922 advanced, HDFC Sky said. The Nifty Midcap 100 rose 0.55% and the Smallcap 100 only 0.10%. The India VIX, a gauge of expected volatility, fell 5.8%.

Money, oil and the rupee

  • Foreign moneyForeign investors bought a net ₹13,749.82 crore of shares on Friday, 6 November, while domestic institutions sold ₹5,968.70 crore, per DSIJ. FPIs have bought more than $4.1 billion of Indian equities so far in November, the strongest monthly inflow since September 2024, HDFC Sky said. See why foreign and domestic funds often trade in opposite directions.
  • Brent crude$97.51 a barrel for January futures at about 3:37 pm, per Kotak Neo, after oil's sharpest weekly fall in more than six years.
  • RupeeRose 5 paise to close at 93.93 to the dollar (provisional), helped by softer crude and foreign inflows, PTI reported. State-run banks bought dollars ahead of the market holiday, which trimmed early gains.
  • AbroadOn Friday the Nasdaq rose 1.09% and the S&P 500 0.31%, while the Dow fell 0.46%. US payrolls rose by 165,000 in October against a forecast of 65,000, and markets put the chance of a Fed hike in December near 80%, per DSIJ.

What to watch

  • HolidayIndian stock markets are closed on Tuesday, 10 November, for Diwali Balipratipada. Trading resumes on Wednesday, 11 November.
  • US inflationUS inflation data due on Tuesday, a key input for the Fed's December decision, PTI noted.
  • Lock-in expiriesAbout $35 billion of shares in 116 companies become free to trade as lock-ins end between 9 November 2026 and 26 February 2027, per Kotak Neo. That can add supply in recently listed stocks.
  • ResultsThe September-quarter earnings season continues. Siemens reported a 4.78% rise in profit to ₹504 crore, per DSIJ.
