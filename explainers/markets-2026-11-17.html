Cheaper oil lifts the rupee 43 paise and the Nifty 96 points to 25,387; PSU banks lead, IT slips before Nvidia

The Nifty rose 0.38% and the Sensex closed at 82,532.19 as Brent fell towards $92 and foreign buyers kept coming. The rupee gained 43 paise to 93.51, PSU banks jumped 1.95% and IT fell 0.9% ahead of Nvidia's results.

Oil fell, foreign money kept coming in and India's market rose for a second day. On 17 November, the Nifty 50 gained 96.30 points, or 0.38%, to 25,387.40. The Sensex rose 427.40 points, or 0.52%, to 82,532.19. The biggest winner of the day, though, was the rupee.

The Nifty touched 25,501.35 during the session before settling about 114 points lower, according to Angel One. It is now at its highest close of the past seven sessions, up from our 16 November update.

A second day up, after a brief look above 25,500

Nifty 50 closing level, last seven trading sessions (markets were shut on 10 November). Source: NSE, via FIN2DAY daily updates, Angel One, HDFC Sky.

Under the surface, the picture was mixed. Inside the Nifty 50, 27 stocks rose and 23 fell. Across the wider market, HDFC Sky counted 2,012 shares rising and 2,148 falling. The Nifty Midcap 100 gained about 0.3% (Kotak Neo put it at 0.25%), while the Nifty Smallcap 100 slipped 0.22%. India VIX, the market's fear gauge, fell about 1% to 15.5, Trendlyne reported. New to the indices? Read how the Sensex and Nifty work.

Cheaper oil, a stronger rupee

Brent crude futures were trading 0.38% lower at $92.79 a barrel in the afternoon, PTI reported. January Brent had settled at $95 on 13 November, Upstox noted. Kotak Neo said shipments through the Strait of Hormuz have nearly returned to pre-war levels. Business Standard reported that US Vice President JD Vance had signalled Washington wants to avoid a prolonged war in Iran. India imports most of its oil, so cheaper crude eases pressure on prices and on the currency.

The rupee rose 43 paise to close at 93.51 to the dollar (provisional), from 93.94 on Monday. Forex traders credited softer crude, firm local shares and foreign inflows, PTI said. Anuj Choudhary of Mirae Asset Sharekhan said a strong dollar and rising US bond yields could cap further gains. For background, see how the RBI defends the rupee.

Foreign investors bought a net ₹4,864.68 crore of Indian shares on Monday, exchange data show, their fifth straight day of buying. HDFC Sky put November's foreign inflows at a record $9.53 billion so far. Domestic institutions sold about ₹3,519 crore, their 12th straight selling day. Read how foreign and domestic flows move the market.

PSU banks and property led, IT lagged

Nifty sectoral and broader indices, percentage change on the day. Nifty Realty, not shown, rose nearly 2%. Source: Kotak Neo, HDFC Sky market wraps.

The Nifty PSU Bank index jumped 1.95%. Punjab National Bank rose 3.74% and State Bank of India 1.66%, HDFC Sky said. Property stocks were also in demand: the Nifty Realty index gained nearly 2%, with Brigade Enterprises up 3.97% and Prestige Estates up 3.22%. HDFC Sky linked the realty buying to expectations of steady housing demand.

Technology went the other way. The Nifty IT index fell 0.90%. Overnight, the US Nasdaq Composite had dropped 2.49% on worries about AI valuations and tighter US curbs on advanced chip sales to China. HCLTech fell 2.09% and Infosys 1.32%.

Top movers

Biggest Nifty 50 gainers and losers, percentage change on the day. Source: Angel One, HDFC Sky (NSE prices).

Adani Enterprises led the Nifty with a 3.24% rise to ₹2,745.10. Eicher Motors gained 3.01% to ₹7,828 after brokers backed the stock, HDFC Sky said. Lower oil prices helped paint makers, and Asian Paints rose 2.02%. On the losing side, state-run energy names fell: Power Grid lost 1.60%, ONGC 1.53% and Coal India 1.50%.

What to watch

  • Nvidia resultsNvidia reports after the US market close on 17 November, its first results since its chief executive's exit. The numbers will test the AI trade that has been pushing money towards India.
  • Fed minutesMinutes of the US Federal Reserve's 27–28 October meeting are due on 18 November.
  • IPOsCrystal Crop Protection's IPO opens on 18 November. Coal India's Mahanadi Coalfields IPO (₹129–₹136 a share) opens on 19 November.
  • Rupee and reservesThe RBI's weekly forex reserves data is due on 20 November. Whether Brent stays near $92 will matter for the rupee.
