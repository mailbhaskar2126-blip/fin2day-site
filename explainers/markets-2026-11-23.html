Sensex sinks 1,483.72 points to 82,435.99 as banks and Tata Steel drag; IT stocks rise against the tide

The Nifty fell 1.75% to 25,349.65 and the Sensex 1.77% to 82,435.99, wiping out the last two days of gains. Bank stocks fell over 3%, Tata Steel slid 8.43% on its APL Apollo deal, and the rupee lost 30 paise to 92.92.

Monday started well. The Nifty opened higher and touched 25,992.60 in early trade, a few points short of 26,000. By the close it had given back everything and more.

The Nifty 50 ended 450.95 points, or 1.75%, lower at 25,349.65. The Sensex fell 1,483.72 points, or 1.77%, to 82,435.99. That erased the gains of Thursday and Friday, covered in our 20 November update.

From near 26,000 to the day's low

Nifty 50 open, high, low and close, in points versus Friday's close of 25,800.60. FIN2DAY calculation. Source: DSIJ, Univest.

The index swung about 649 points from top to bottom and closed only 5.80 points above its low of 25,343.85. A close near the low means selling kept going into the final minutes.

Three things drove it, according to the closing reports. First, profit booking: the indices had gained more than 11% in November before today, Univest noted. Second, heavy selling in banks and metals. Third, the monthly derivatives expiry, moved to Monday because of Tuesday's holiday, which made trading choppier. The India VIX, a gauge of expected volatility, rose 4.04% to 18.02.

Global cues were weak too. On Friday, the S&P 500 fell 3.70% and the Nasdaq Composite 5.28% on worries about AI valuations, DSIJ reported. In Asia on Monday morning, Japan's Nikkei 225 was down 5.33% and South Korea's Kospi 3.64%, India TV reported.

Banks and metals led the fall

Nifty sector index moves, % change on the day. Financials is Nifty Financial Services. Source: DSIJ, Univest.

The Nifty Bank index fell 3.17% to 62,585.35, dropping 2,049.85 points, Univest data show. Private banks (-3.19%) and PSU banks (-3.25%) fell almost equally. Axis Bank, HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank were among the five worst Nifty stocks.

The broader market fell less. The Nifty Midcap 100 lost 1.01% to 61,403.20 and the Smallcap 100 0.66% to 19,314.40, according to Upstox.

Tata Steel's big bet

The single biggest drag was Tata Steel, down 8.43% to ₹163. It announced a deal with APL Apollo Tubes. Tata Steel will buy an initial 26.38% stake for about ₹15,511 crore. That triggers an open offer for another 26%, worth up to ₹16,756 crore. Separately, APL Apollo will buy Tata Steel's tubes and colour-coated steel businesses for up to ₹28,000 crore, subject to approvals, DSIJ reported. APL Apollo's own shares fell 21.92%, Upstox data show. Hindalco's 3.13% fall added to the pressure on metals.

IT went the other way. The Nifty IT rose 1.69%, led by TCS (+3.53%), Tech Mahindra and HCLTech. Investors see Indian IT services as less exposed to the global AI selloff than chipmakers, Kotak Neo reported. Tech Mahindra has also set 30 November as the record date for a 1:1 bonus issue. Shriram Finance (+2.46%) was the top non-IT gainer.

Biggest Nifty 50 gainers and losers, % change on the day. Tata Steel (−8.43%) is covered above and left out so the other bars stay readable. Source: DSIJ, Univest.

Rupee ends a five-day rally

The rupee fell 30 paise to a provisional 92.92 to the dollar, PTI reported. It had closed at 92.62 on Friday, five days into a rally. It opened at 92.47 and touched 93.11 at its weakest. Lower crude and a softer dollar could not offset the selloff in stocks. Brent crude was at $92.08 a barrel, down 0.53% in futures trade, and the dollar index was 103.69.

Foreign investors had been the market's main support. They bought a net ₹10,308.42 crore of Indian shares on Friday, provisional exchange data quoted by PTI show. Whether that buying holds after Monday's fall is the key question. For background, read why FPI and DII flows matter.
