Sensex sinks 989 points to 83,339.26 as IT gives back gains and Brent nears $97; rupee falls 60 paise

The Nifty fell 1.07% to 25,607.20 and the Sensex 1.17% to 83,339.26 as IT stocks reversed Thursday's rally and a Houthi attack on a Saudi oil facility lifted crude. Midcaps, smallcaps and metals still rose.

A two-day rally ended on Friday. The Nifty 50 fell 277.30 points, or 1.07%, to 25,607.20. The Sensex lost 989.33 points, or 1.17%, to 83,339.26.

Two things did the damage: IT stocks gave back Thursday's big gain, and crude oil jumped after a Houthi attack on a Saudi Aramco facility. Yet smaller companies quietly rose, and metal stocks had a good day.

A week's gains, mostly gone

Nifty 50 closing level, last four sessions (24 Nov was a market holiday). Source: NSE via Upstox, Liquide, HDFC Sky; earlier FIN2DAY updates.

The Nifty opened about 45 points lower at 25,839 and touched 25,909 early on, according to Liquide. Selling grew through the afternoon and it hit a low of 25,565.90, per Upstox. The close wiped out the gains of Thursday and part of Wednesday's rebound, covered in our 26 November update.

The Sensex fell as far as 83,208.33 during the day. Bank Nifty slipped 494.40 points, or 0.78%, to 63,040.90. India VIX, the market's fear gauge, eased 1.90% to 16.52.

Big names fell, small ones rose

This was a narrow sell-off. The Nifty Midcap 100 rose 0.10% to 61,846.30 and the Nifty Smallcap 100 gained 0.56% to 19,481.30, per Upstox. The losses were concentrated in large IT, auto and financial stocks, which carry a lot of weight in the Nifty and Sensex. Our explainer on how the Sensex and Nifty work shows why a few heavyweights can move the whole index.

Nifty sector and broader indices, % change on 27 November 2026. Source: Liquide, Upstox, Marketcalls.
  • IT −1.88%The worst sector. IT had jumped 3.66% on Thursday, and traders booked profits. HCL Tech fell 3.03%, Infosys 2.68% and TCS 2.57%.
  • Auto −1.48%Autos fell for a second day. Bajaj Auto dropped 3.36%.
  • Financials −1.33%Shriram Finance was the biggest Nifty loser, down 3.99%. Marketcalls reports the company called market talk about an RBI audit untrue.
  • Metal +1.40%The best sector. JSW Steel rose 1.40% and Hindalco 0.82%.

Who moved the index

Top five Nifty 50 gainers and losers, % change on 27 November 2026. Source: Upstox.

Defence stocks were a bright spot. Bharat Electronics (BEL) led the Nifty with a 1.72% gain. Among smaller stocks, BEML jumped 13.67% and Bharat Dynamics 4.13%, Upstox reported.

Oil is back above $97

Brent crude was trading at $97.19 a barrel, up 0.64%, around the time the rupee closed, PTI reported. Analysts linked the rise to the Houthis attacking an Aramco facility in Saudi Arabia and to reports of ships being targeted in the Strait of Hormuz.

The National reported that the strike hit the Jazan refinery on Saudi Arabia's Red Sea coast and temporarily disrupted operations, and that Brent was heading for a weekly gain of about 7%, its first weekly rise this month. India imports most of its crude, so higher oil means a bigger import bill and more demand for dollars. Our explainer on what $100 oil means for India walks through why.

Rupee drops 60 paise

The rupee fell 60 paise to 92.98 per dollar (provisional), from Thursday's 92.38, PTI reported. It touched 93.01 at its weakest. Dealers pointed to costlier oil, a firm dollar and month-end dollar demand from importers. The dollar index was up 0.16% at 103.77.

Foreign money is still coming in

Foreign institutional investors bought a net ₹5,843.33 crore of Indian shares on Thursday, per exchange data cited by PTI, and PTI cites record foreign equity inflows of $15.16 billion in November. That support did not stop Friday's fall, but it helps explain why the market has held up this month. See our explainer on foreign and domestic investor flows.
