Questions

Good to know.

Is this investment advice?

No. FIN2DAY explains what happened and why. We never recommend buying or selling anything. For decisions about your money, talk to a SEBI-registered adviser.

How often do you post?

Every day. Monday is money basics, Tuesday IPOs, Wednesday the RBI and rates, Thursday the global economy and Friday trade wars. After every trading day there is a markets update, and on weekends we cover world markets like the NYSE and Shanghai. When big news breaks, we cover it the same day.

Is it free?

Yes. The Instagram page, the site and the newsletter are all free.

Where do the numbers come from?

From primary sources like the RBI, SEBI, government releases, and established news agencies. Every explainer lists its sources at the bottom.

Can I suggest a topic?

Please do. Use the contact form or DM us on Instagram.
