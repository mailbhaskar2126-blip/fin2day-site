Asia sinks, India rises: Sensex +898, Nifty 25,800; rupee 92.67

The Nifty rose 1.12% to 25,800.60 and the Sensex 897.96 points to 83,919.71, even as Japan and South Korea fell more than 5% in an AI-stock selloff. Bank stocks led, foreign buying continued, and the rupee gained 65 paise to 92.67.
