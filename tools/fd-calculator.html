How it is worked out

Maturity = P × (1 + r ÷ (100 × k))k × t, where k is the number of compounding periods a year (4 for quarterly, the common bank FD practice) and t is the number of years. Interest is shown before tax.

Educational estimate only. It assumes a constant rate for the whole period; real returns and rates change. Nothing here is investment advice.
