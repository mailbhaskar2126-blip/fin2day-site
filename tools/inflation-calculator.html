Inflation calculator

What something costing you today could cost in the future, or what today's money is worth then.

₹
%
years

How it is worked out

Future cost = today's cost × (1 + i)n, where i is the yearly inflation rate and n is the number of years. Purchasing power of today's amount = today's amount ÷ (1 + i)n.

Educational estimate only. It assumes a constant rate for the whole period; real returns and rates change. Nothing here is investment advice.
