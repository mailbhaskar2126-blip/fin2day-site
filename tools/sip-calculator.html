How it is worked out

Future value = P × [((1 + i)n − 1) ÷ i] × (1 + i), where P is the monthly amount, i is the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12) and n is the number of months. Instalments are assumed to be paid at the start of each month.

Educational estimate only. It assumes a constant rate for the whole period; real returns and rates change. Nothing here is investment advice.
